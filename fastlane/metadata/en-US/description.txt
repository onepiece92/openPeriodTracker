Does it work offline? Yes. Every feature runs on your phone, with no server behind it.
Do I need an account? No. Open the app and start logging.
What does it cost? Nothing. No subscription, no premium tier, no ads.

Open Period Tracker is a free, open-source period and cycle tracker for people who want useful insights without handing their health data to anyone.

TRACK
• Log periods and flow (light, medium, heavy) on a calendar
• Record moods, symptoms and notes for any day
• Doctor's checklist: pain, discharge, skin, hair, sleep, libido, digestion, breast changes, energy, weight
• Period history and a comparison of the current cycle against your average

PREDICT
• Next period, fertile window and peak day, shown on the calendar and home screen
• An on-device model refines cycle length and period duration predictions from your own history
• 7-day phase forecast and symptom forecast based on your past cycles
• Optional reminders before a predicted period

UNDERSTAND
• Insights: best and worst cycle days, weekly logging score, phase-based tips drawn from your own logs
• Analytics: cycle length trends, mood and symptom frequency, phase correlations
• Diet & Nutrition: phase-aware foods, macro and hydration guides, supplement suggestions, and regional food picks if you choose to share your location
• Yoga: poses and breathwork matched to each cycle phase, with links to practice videos
• AI prompt builder: create an anonymised summary of your logs to paste into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini if you want a second opinion

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your data lives in a local database on your device
• No account, no cloud, no analytics, no advertising SDKs
• Back up and restore with a JSON file you control
• Location is optional and is used only to pick a food region
• Optional: point the app at your own Ollama server for local AI summaries
